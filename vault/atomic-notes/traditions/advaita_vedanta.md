# Advaita Vedānta

Advaita Vedānta is the non-dual school of Hindu philosophy, founded by Śaṅkara (788–820 CE). It holds that ultimate reality is Brahman, and the apparent world of plurality (māyā) is a superimposition (ādhyāsa) on the non-dual Brahman. The individual ātman is ultimately identical with Brahman—what appears as "rebirth" is the continuation of avidyā (ignorance).

## Cross-Tradition Notes
Advaita presents the most radical challenge to reincarnation: if only Brahman exists, there is no "soul" that transmigrates—only the appearance of separation caused by ignorance. Liberation is recognition, not escape.
