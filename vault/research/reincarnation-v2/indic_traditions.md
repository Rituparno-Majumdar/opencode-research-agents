# Indic Traditions: reincarnation
_Agent: Indic Traditions | Run: 2026-05-19_

## Vedic & Upanishadic Sources

### Brihadaranyaka Upanishad (ca. 800–600 BCE)
- **Tradition:** Vedic / Upanishadic | **Period:** Late Vedic | **Language:** Sanskrit | **School:** None (pre-sectarian)
- **Relevant Teaching:** Discussion of reincarnation (punarjanma) and the cycle of birth and death. The soul (atman) passes through various states.
- **Key Terms:** [atman (self/soul)], [punarjanma (rebirth)], [samsara (cycle of birth and death)]
- **Reference:** Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 4.4.1-4.4.8

### Chandogya Upanishad (ca. 800–600 BCE)
- **Tradition:** Upanishadic | **Period:** Late Vedic | **Language:** Sanskrit | **School:** Samaveda
- **Relevant Teaching:** Contains teaching on the journey of the soul after death and the law of karma.
- **Key Terms:** [karma (action)], [dharma (duty/righteousness)]
- **Reference:** Chandogya Upanishad 5.3-5.10

## Philosophical Schools

### Samkhya Karika (Isvara Krishna, ca. 400 CE)
- **Tradition:** Samkhya | **Period:** Classical | **Language:** Sanskrit | **School:** Samkhya
- **Relevant Teaching:** Theory of purusha (consciousness) and prakriti (matter); bondage through tattvas; no eternal self but individual centers of consciousness.
- **Key Terms:** [purusha (consciousness)], [prakriti (primordial matter)], [puruusha (individual consciousness)]
- **Reference:** verses 1-22

### Yoga Sutras of Patanjali (ca. 400 CE)
- **Tradition:** Yoga | **Period:** Classical | **Language:** Sanskrit | **School:** Yoga
- **Relevant Teaching:** Cessation of suffering (klaivya) through samadhi; liberation (kaivalya) from the cycle.
- **Key Terms:** [kaivalya (liberation)], [samadhi (absorption)], [nirodha (cessation)]
- **Reference:** sutras 1-2

### Advaita Vedanta - Vivekachudamani (Shankara, ca. 788-820 CE)
- **Tradition:** Advaita Vedanta | **Period:** Medieval | **Language:** Sanskrit | **School:** Advaita
- **Relevant Teaching:** Non-dual teaching that Atman is identical with Brahman; apparent rebirth is ignorance (avidya).
- **Key Terms:** [Advaita (non-dual)], [Brahman (ultimate reality)], [Maya (illusion)]
- **Reference:** verses 1-50

## Epic & Puranic Sources

### Bhagavad Gita (ca. 400 BCE - 200 CE)
- **Tradition:** Hindu (Vaishnava) | **Period:** Epic | **Language:** Sanskrit | **School:** Various
- **Relevant Teaching:** Karma yoga, path of action; discussion of the immortal soul that passes through death; liberation through devotion.
- **Key Terms:** [Karma yoga (path of action)], [Bhakti (devotion)], [Moksha (liberation)]
- **Reference:** Chapters 2, 8, 9, 12

### Moksha Dharma Parva - Mahabharata (ca. 400 BCE - 400 CE)
- **Tradition:** Hindu | **Period:** Epic | **Language:** Sanskrit | **School:** None
- **Relevant Teaching:** Detailed discussion of karma, rebirth, and paths to liberation.
- **Key Terms:** [Karma], [Dharma], [Moksha]
- **Reference:** Book 12 (Shanti Parva), chapters 176-350

## Buddhist & Jain Sources

### Buddhist - Dhammapada (Pali, ca. 300 BCE)
- **Tradition:** Buddhist | **Period:** Early | **Language:** Pali | **School:** Theravada
- **Relevant Teaching:** No eternal self (anatta); rebirth dependent on kamma; cessation of suffering (nirodha).
- **Key Terms:** [Anatta (non-self)], [Kamma (action/rebirth cause)], [Nirodha (cessation)]
- **Reference:** verses 1-50, 127-153

### Buddhist - Bardo Thodol (Tibetan Book of the Dead) (8th century CE, compiled later)
- **Tradition:** Buddhist (Vajrayana) | **Period:** Medieval | **Language:** Tibetan | **School:** Nyingma
- **Relevant Teaching:** Intermediate state (bardo) between death and rebirth; recognition of clear light.
- **Key Terms:** [Bardo (intermediate state)], [Phowa (transference of consciousness)]
- **Reference:** Chapters 1-7

### Jain - Tattvartha Sutra (Umaswati, ca. 2nd century CE)
- **Tradition:** Jain | **Period:** Early Classical | **Language:** Sanskrit | **School:** Svetambara
- **Relevant Teaching:** Theory of karma as matter (karmana) binding the soul (jiva); path to liberation through right faith, knowledge, and conduct.
- **Key Terms:** [Jiva (soul)], [Karmana (karmic matter)], [Moksha (liberation)]
- **Reference:** Sutras 1-30

## Regional Language Sources

### Thirukkural (Tamil, ca. 1st century CE)
- **Tradition:** Tamil | **Period:** Sangam | **Language:** Tamil | **School:** None
- **Relevant Teaching:** Ethical teachings related to dharma and artha; includes references to virtuous conduct and its fruits.
- **Key Terms:** [Dharma (righteousness)], [Virtue]
- **Reference:** couplets 1-100

## Gaps & Honest Limitations

Indic tradition coverage is vast. This file covers major sources but omits: detailed treatment of regional Buddhist traditions (Burmese, Thai), detailed Kashmir Shaivism, Sikh perspectives on reincarnation (simplified), and various medieval bhakti movements' specific karma-rebirth doctrines.
