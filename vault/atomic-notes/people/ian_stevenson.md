# Ian Stevenson

Ian Stevenson (1918–2007) was a psychiatrist who established the Division of Perceptual Studies at the University of Virginia and conducted the most systematic empirical investigation of reincarnation claims in academic history. His research documented thousands of cases of children claiming past-life memories, particularly from South Asia.
