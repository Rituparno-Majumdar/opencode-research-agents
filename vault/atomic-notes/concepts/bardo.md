# Bardo

Bardo is the Tibetan Buddhist concept of an intermediate state between death and rebirth. The "Bardo Thodol" (Tibetan Book of the Dead) describes various bardo states the consciousness encounters after death, including encounters with dharma faces—appearances of Buddha-forms that guide toward liberation or entrap in rebirth.

## Cross-Tradition Notes
Unlike Indian Buddhist thought which focuses on immediate rebirth, Tibetan Buddhism developed elaborate bardo (intermediate state) teachings. This represents a significant elaboration beyond early Buddhist eschatology.
