# Karma

The principle that every action generates corresponding consequences in this or future lives.

## Causal Links (A → B)
- [[karma]] → [[samsara]] — ethical actions shape the cycle of rebirth
- [[karma]] → [[moksha]] — accumulation of good karma can lead to liberation

## Effect Links (A ← B)
- [[atman]] ← [[karma]] — the soul carries the weight of its actions

## Symmetric Links (A ↔ B)
- [[karma]] ↔ [[dharma]] — action and duty are intertwined
