## Quantum Physics

### Quantum Mechanics Fundamentals
- **Type:** [Experimental + Theoretical]
- **Key Equations/Concepts:** Schrödinger equation, wave function (Ψ), superposition, entanglement
- **Relevant Research:** Double-slit experiment, Bell's theorem experiments (Aspect 1982), quantum decoherence studies (Zurek 2003)
- **Consensus Status:** [Established — one of the most precisely tested theories in physics]

### Interpretations of Quantum Mechanics
- **Type:** [Theoretical — empirically equivalent]
- **Key Interpretations:**
  - **Copenhagen** (Bohr, Heisenberg): Wave function is a probability amplitude; collapse occurs upon measurement. Consciousness plays a role (measurement problem).
  - **Many Worlds/Everett** (1957): No collapse — all outcomes occur in branching universes. Consciousness is an emergent pattern.
  - **Bohmian/De Broglie** (1952): Hidden variables + pilot wave. Non-local. Consciousness not directly relevant.
  - **Decoherence** (Zurek): Environment causes apparent collapse — quantum to classical transition
- **Consensus Status:** [Debated — no empirical way to distinguish interpretations]

### Measurement Problem
- **Type:** [Theoretical — unresolved]
- **Key Question:** Why does measurement cause wave function collapse? What counts as "measurement"? Does consciousness collapse the wave function (von Neumann, Wigner)?
- **Relevant Research:** Quantum Zeno effect, delayed choice experiments (Wheeler)
- **Consensus Status:** [Debated — "measurement" is not physically defined]

## Quantum Consciousness [flag as contested]

### Penrose-Hameroff Orch-OR
- **Proponents:** Roger Penrose (physicist), Stuart Hameroff (anesthesiologist)
- **Critics:** Max Tegmark, Christof Koch, many neuroscientists
- **Key Argument:** Microtubules (neuronal structures) maintain quantum coherence. When superposed states reach Planck-scale energy, quantum gravity causes objective reduction (OR) — this is consciousness.
- **Reception:** [Mainstream consensus: REJECTED — decoherence times in warm, wet brain are ~10^-13 seconds, far too short for quantum computation]

### Bohm — Implicate Order
- **Proponents:** David Bohm (physicist), Karl Pribram (neuropsychologist)
- **Key Argument:** The "implicate order" is a deeper level of reality where consciousness and matter are folded together. The "explicate order" is what we experience. Order unfolds from implicate to explicate.
- **Reception:** [Minority view — philosophically interesting but empirically unfalsifiable]

### Henry Stapp — Mind-Matter Integration
- **Proponents:** Henry Stapp (quantum physicist)
- **Key Argument:** Quantum Zeno effect allows mind to influence brain. Consciousness "collapses" the wave function in the brain.
- **Reception:** [Contested — no empirical support]
