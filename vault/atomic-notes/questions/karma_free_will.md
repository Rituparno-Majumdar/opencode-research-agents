# Karma and Free Will

Is there genuine freedom within the karmic system? Can past karma be overcome?

## Evidence from Research

- Indic traditions: Karma is cumulative but intent matters — past karma is not absolute (especially in Buddhism)
- Western philosophy: Kant's radical freedom vs. Stoic determinism
- The tension: If karma determines rebirth, where is the space for choice?

## Why This Matters

Understanding whether karma allows for transformation or is deterministic affects:
- Whether spiritual practice can lead to liberation
- The meaning of ethical action
- The possibility of change
