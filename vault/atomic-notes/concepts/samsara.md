# Saṃsāra

Saṃsāra refers to the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth in Hindu, Buddhist, and Jain philosophical traditions. The term literally means "wandering" or "continuous flow," describing the soul's passage through various states of existence driven by accumulated karma.

## Cross-Tradition Notes
In Hindu traditions, saṃsāra involves soul (ātman) transmigration. In Buddhist doctrine, saṃsāra operates without permanent self—anattā. This represents fundamental divergence on what passes through the cycle.
