# Bhagavad Gītā

The Bhagavad Gītā (c. 5th–2nd century BCE) presents the definitive synthesis of paths to liberation from rebirth: karma yoga (path of action), jñāna yoga (path of knowledge), and bhakti yoga (path of devotion). Key verses (2.20-25) establish the soul as unborn, eternal, and imperishable—"na jāyate mriyate vā kadācin."

## Cross-Tradition Notes
The Gītā's antakāla doctrine—that the final thought at death determines the next birth—became central to later Śaiva and devotional traditions. It provides the practical teaching on which rebirth depends.
