# Moksha

Moksha is liberation from the cycle of saṃsāra—the ultimate goal in Hindu, Buddhist, and Jain traditions. It represents the cessation of rebirth and the realization of one's true nature, whether as Brahman (Advaita), union with Vishnu (Vaishnavism), or nibbāna (Buddhism).

## Cross-Tradition Notes
In Advaita Vedanta, moksha is recognition that ātman is already Brahman—no actual "rebirth" to escape, only illusion to see through. In Buddhism, nibbāna is the cessation of conditional existence.
