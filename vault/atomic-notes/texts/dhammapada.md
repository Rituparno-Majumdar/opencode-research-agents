# Dhammapada

The Dhammapada is a fundamental Buddhist scripture containing 423 verses organized around themes. Chapter 1 establishes the ethical foundation for rebirth: "With mind as chief, all dhammas are mind-made. If one speaks or acts with a corrupt mind, suffering follows as the wheel follows the ox that pulls the cart."

## Cross-Tradition Notes
The Dhammapada emphasizes the centrality of mental intention (cetanā) in kamma—the ethical dimension of Buddhist karma differs from Hindu ritual-centered karma.
