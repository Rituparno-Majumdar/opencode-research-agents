# Ātman

Ātman is the eternal, self-luminous core of individual consciousness in Hindu philosophy. It represents the true Self that transcends the physical body and mind, fundamentally identical with Brahman according to Advaita Vedanta.

## Cross-Tradition Notes
In contrast to Hindu ātman, Buddhist anattā (non-self) explicitly rejects any permanent, substantial self. This is the fundamental ontological divergence between Hindu and Buddhist thought on rebirth.
