# Orchestrator Review: Karma Research

## Executive Summary

This research run examined कर्म (karma) — action and moral causation — across four scholarly traditions. The four-agent approach produced comprehensive coverage of the Indic concept across Hindu, Buddhist, Jain, and philosophical traditions, with significant comparative data from Western philosophy, ancient civilizations, and contemporary scholarship. ## Source Evaluation

### Indic Traditions Agent — 9/10

**Strengths:**
- Excellent coverage of six major traditions: Vedānta, Sāṅkhya-Yoga, Buddhism, Jainism, Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika, Tamil traditions
- Proper use of Devanagari script throughout: कर्म (karma), अविद्या (avidyā), सांसार (saṃsāra)
- Accurate citation of primary sources: Upanishads, Brahmasūtra, Abhidharma, Jain Āgamas
- Strong causal chain diagrams (karma → birth → desire)
- Detailed Jain karmavāda with eight karma types

**Gaps/Issues:**
- Some Tamil source names in transliteration without script
- Colonial influence on karma concept only briefly mentioned

### Western Philosophy Agent — 8.5/10

**Strengths:**
- Clear parallel structure: moira vs. karma, hexis vs. samskāra
- Excellent Stoic treatment (logos as immanent principle)
- Schopenhauer as key bridge between Indic and Western
- Process philosophy addition (Whitehead) provides contemporary synthesis

**Gaps/Issues:**
- Existentialism (Sartre) would strengthen the "radical freedom vs. karma" tension
- Medieval Christian theology underaddressed

### Ancient Civilizations Agent — 8/10

**Strengths:**
- Strong Egyptian ma'at treatment with clear distinction from karma
- Excellent cross-cultural comparison table
- Chinese and Mesopotamian coverage complete

**Gaps/Issues:**
- Celtic/Norse rebirth evidence not included
- Mayan/Aztec exploration only surface-level

### Contemporary Scholarship Agent — 8/10

**Strengths:**
- Doniger translation problem clearly articulated
- Reincarnation research (Stevenson, Tucker) properly flagged as controversial
- Cognitive science adds valuable "why karma works" dimension

**Gaps/Issues:**
- More peer-reviewed anthropological studies needed
- "Quantum karma" pseudo-science mentioned but not critiqued strongly enough

### Science & Technology Agent — Not Dispatched

Correct decision. Karma is a philosophical/religious concept with no significant physics dimension.

## Notable Convergences

1. **Karmic causation = internal vs. external**: Indic karma is internally generated; Greek moira is externally decreed
2. **Stoic Logos ≈ Sāṅkhya prakṛti**: Both are immanent rational principles
3. **Schopenhauer's Will ≈ Vedānta ātman**: Both are metaphysical ground, but with opposite valuations
4. **Jain karmavāda = most elaborate**: No other tradition matches Jain detail of karma-particles
