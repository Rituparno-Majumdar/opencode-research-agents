# Plato

Plato (428–348 BCE) presented the most philosophically developed reincarnation-like framework in Greek thought, particularly in the Phaedo, Republic (Myth of Er), and Phaedrus. His doctrine of anamnesis (recollection) holds that the soul recalls knowledge acquired in its pre-incarnate contemplation of the Forms.

## Cross-Tradition Notes
Plato presents reincarnation as myth rather than dogmatic teaching. The Myth of Er describes souls choosing their next lives after death—a cosmic lottery of moral accountability that parallels Indian karma while differing in mechanism.
