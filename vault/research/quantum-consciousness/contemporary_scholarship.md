## Consciousness Studies & NDE Research
### Penrose-Hameroff — Orch-OR Theory
- **Author:** Roger Penrose, Stuart Hameroff | **Year:** 1994-present | **Publisher/Journal:** Physics of Life Reviews, Journal of Consciousness Studies
- **Type:** [PEER-REVIEWED] | **Methodology:** Theoretical physics + neuroscience | **Traditions Covered:** Physics, Neuroscience, Philosophy
- **Key Contribution:** Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR) posits that consciousness arises from quantum gravitational effects in brain microtubules. When superposed quantum states reach a threshold (objective reduction), they collapse — this collapse IS consciousness.
- **Key Physics:** Objective reduction (OR) — quantum gravity causes wave function collapse at ~10^7 seconds
- **Key Neuroscience:** Microtubules — structural proteins in neurons
- [CONTESTED: widely cited, strongly rejected by mainstream neuroscience]

### Integrated Information Theory — Phi
- **Author:** Giulio Tononi | **Year:** 2004-present | **Publisher/Journal:** Cerebral Cortex, PLoS Computational Biology
- **Type:** [PEER-REVIEWED] | **Methodology:** Theoretical neuroscience | **Traditions Covered:** Neuroscience, Information Theory
- **Key Contribution:** Phi (Φ) measures integrated information — the "quantity of experience." Consciousness is identical to integrated information. This is not quantum — it uses classical information theory.
- [CONTESTED: widely discussed, mathematically precise but empirically untested]

### Global Workspace Theory
- **Author:** Bernard Baars | **Year:** 1988-present | **Publisher/Journal:** Cognition, Trends in Cognitive Sciences
- **Type:** [PEER-REVIEWED] | **Methodology:** Cognitive neuroscience | **Traditions Covered:** Cognitive Science, Neuroscience
- **Key Contribution:** Consciousness is "global broadcast" — information becomes conscious when it enters the global workspace and is available to multiple brain systems. This is a classical (non-quantum) theory.
- [ACCEPTED: widely supported by empirical evidence]

## Gaps & Honest Limitations
1. **Mainstream consensus**: The vast majority of neuroscientists reject quantum consciousness. The Orch-OR theory has been criticized as mathematically implausible (e.g., Max Tegmark's calculation that quantum coherence in brain lasts only 10^-13 seconds)
2. **Physics vs. metaphor**: Many "quantum consciousness" claims are metaphorical — not actual physics
3. **Empirical evidence**: There is NO empirical evidence for quantum effects in brain cognition — all claims are theoretical
4. **AI consciousness**: Whether any current or near-future AI can be conscious is an open empirical question — we have no "consciousness detector"
