## Cross-Tradition Analysis

### Productive Connections Identified

1. **Stoic apatheia ↔ Sāṅkhya-Yoga kaivalya**: Both emphasize liberation from passion through correct understanding. Western agent correctly identifies this parallel while noting the multi-life framework difference.

2. **Schopenhauer's Will denial ↔ Buddhist nirvāṇa and Sāṅkhya-Yoga**: The strongest Western parallel to Indian liberation. Schopenhauer's explicit acknowledgment of Upanishad influence makes this a genuine intellectual lineage, not mere analogy.

3. **Eleusinian mysteries ↔ Yoga sādhana**: Both involve initiation, specialized knowledge, and transformation. Ancient Civilizations agent correctly notes the post-mortem vs. liberation-from-rebirth difference.

4. **Zoroastrian Frashokereti ↔ Hindu liberation as cosmic restoration**: Both involve collective eschatological transformation, though the frameworks differ significantly.

5. **Contemporary Heidegger ↔ Advaita Vedānta**: Reich's comparison (Contemporary Scholarship, line 31-32) identifies the "falling" parallel while noting crucial divergence between recovery vs. projection.

### Critical Divergences Highlighted

The review correctly identifies three fundamental differences that prevent full translation:

1. **Cosmological framework**: saṃsāra-karma complex absent in Western/ancient traditions
2. **Metaphysical outcome**: Western preservation of individual identity vs. Indian dissolution into brahman
3. **Path structure**: This-worldly single-lifetime vs. multi-life spiritual practice
