# Metempsychosis

Metempsychosis is the Greek philosophical term for the transmigration of souls between bodies. Most prominently associated with Pythagoras and Plato, it posits that the soul undergoes a cycle of incarnations, passing through different animal and human bodies in accordance with moral logic.

## Cross-Tradition Notes
While Greek metempsychosis shares the cyclical rebirth structure with Indian saṃsāra, it lacks the karmic causation mechanism. The soul's subsequent incarnations reflect moral purification but without the detailed karma system.
