## Death Studies & Thanatology

### "Twenty Cases Suggestive of Reincarnation" (Ian Stevenson, 1966, revised 1997)
- **Author:** Ian Stevenson | **Year:** 1966 (revised 1997) | **Publisher:** University of Virginia Press | **Type:** [PEER-REVIEWED]
- **Methodology:** Empirical investigation of children's claims of past-life memories; cross-cultural cases; verification of statements against historical records
- **Traditions Covered:** Indian, Burmese, Thai, Lebanese, American cases
- **Key Contribution:** Systematic documentation of 2,500+ cases over 40 years; statistical analysis; argued for reincarnation as best explanation for some cases
- **Controversy:** Criticized for selection bias, inadequate controls, cultural assumptions

### "Children Who Remember Past Lives" (Ian Stevenson, 2000)
- **Author:** Ian Stevenson | **Year:** 2000 | **Publisher:** McFarland & Company | **Type:** [PEER-REVIEWED]
- **Methodology:** Case studies; analysis of birthmarks/correspondence to alleged previous death
- **Key Contribution:** Expanded database; new cases from diverse cultures
- **Gaps:** No mechanism proposed; no peer-reviewed replication

### "European Cases Suggestive of Reincarnation" (Ian Stevenson, 2003)
- **Author:** Ian Stevenson | **Year:** 2003 | **Publisher:** University of Virginia Press | **Type:** [PEER-REVIEWED]
- **Methodology:** Western cases (British, German, Dutch)
- **Key Contribution:** Addresses criticism of cultural bias; 20+ European cases
- **Note:** Fewer strong cases than Indian collection

## Near-Death Experience Research

### Raymond Moody - "Life After Life" (1975)
- **Author:** Raymond Moody | **Year:** 1975 | **Publisher:** Harper & Row | **Type:** [POPULAR]
- **Methodology:** Interviews with 150+ survivors of near-death experiences
- **Traditions Covered:** Western (American)
- **Key Contribution:** Popularized NDE phenomenon; described "tunnel," "light," "life review"
- **Critique:** Anecdotal; no systematic controls; popularized rather than peer-reviewed

### "Near-death experience in survivors of cardiac arrest" (Pim van Lommel et al., The Lancet, 2001)
- **Author:** Pim van Lommel, Wanabe van Leeuwen, Corine Vissers | **Year:** 2001 | **Journal:** The Lancet 358:2039-2045 | **Type:** [PEER-REVIEWED]
- **Methodology:** Prospective study; 344 cardiac arrest survivors; interviews; matched controls
- **Traditions Covered:** Dutch hospital patients
- **Key Findings:** 18% reported NDE; frequency correlated with duration of unconsciousness NOT oxygen levels; depth of NDE correlated with future wellbeing
- **Academic Standing:** [CONTESTED: widely cited but criticized for selection bias and no mechanism]

### Sam Parnia et al. - "AWARE Study" (Resuscitation, 2014)
- **Author:** Sam Parnia et al. | **Year:** 2014 | **Journal:** Resuscitation 85:1799-1805 | **Type:** [PEER-REVIEWED]
- **Methodology:** Prospective study; 2060 cardiac arrest patients; 40 hospitals; hidden target cards in ICU
- **Traditions Covered:** UK, US, Australia hospitals
- **Key Findings:** 2% reported awareness during CPR; 1 case with veridical perception of target; limited evidence for out-of-body perception
- **Academic Standing:** [CONTESTED: findings weak, no veridical verification of target]

## Philosophical Critics

### Paul Edwards - "Reincarnation: A Critical Examination" (1996)
- **Author:** Paul Edwards | **Year:** 1996 | **Publisher:** Prometheus Books | **Type:** [TRADE SCHOLARLY]
- **Methodology:** Philosophical critique; logical analysis of reincarnation claims
- **Key Contribution:** The "replication argument": if souls reincarnate, where are all the infant souls with memories of past lives who can be verified? Statistical argument against reincarnation.
- **Critique Response:** Stevenson argued some children do report verifiable past-life memories; critics respond these are explained by fraud, cryptomnesia, cultural transmission

## Academic Debates and Methodology

### Methodology Issues in NDE Research

- Selection bias: Who reports? Who remembers?
- Retroactive memory construction: Interviews months/years later
- Cultural expectations: Different cultures report different NDE content
- No causal mechanism identified: Correlation NOT causation
- No physical evidence: Only subjective reports

### Methodology Issues in Stevenson Research

- Selection bias: Only unusual cases investigated
- Alternative explanations: Cryptomnesia (forgotten learning), fraud, confabulation, parental suggestion
- No peer-reviewed replication: Despite thousands of cases, none independently verified
- Verification problems: Historical records in some cultures more complete than others

### Psychological Explanations

- Oxygen deprivation: Brain hypoxia during cardiac arrest
- Endorphin release: Natural painkiller during trauma
- REM intrusion: Sleep paralysis and hallucinations
- Cultural conditioning: Familiar afterlife imagery

### Parapsychology Mainstream Status

- Mainstream science considers reincarnation evidence "anecdotal"
- No peer-reviewed, replicated evidence meeting scientific standards
- Field considered "fringe" by most scientists
- Some philosophers take some claims seriously as philosophical puzzles
